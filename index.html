Privacy Policy: Telegram TV Pro

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Telegram TV Pro ("the app") is published by Bakr Mohamed Hassan (bakr.m.hassan@outlook.com).

What the app does with your data

The app connects to your own Telegram account, through Telegram's official client library (TDLib), so it can list the channels you have joined and play the videos in them.

  • Sign-in. You sign in by scanning a QR code with the Telegram app on your phone, so no password is needed. If your account has two-step verification, the password you type is passed straight to Telegram and is not stored by the app.
  • Stored on your TV. Your Telegram session, the chat and file lists Telegram sends, and videos while you watch them are stored on the TV. Cached videos are cleaned up automatically and you can clear them in Settings. Signing out in the app, or ending the session in Telegram under Settings > Devices, removes access.
  • Your preferences (library, watch progress, quality choice) are stored on the TV only.

What we do not do

  • We do not run a server that receives your Telegram data, messages, files or watch history.
  • We do not include advertising or analytics.
  • We do not sell or share your data.

Network connections

The app talks to Telegram's servers (to sign in and download what you play). It does not connect to any other server.

Content

The app shows videos from channels you chose to join. We do not host, select or review that content. You are responsible for having the right to watch it.

Your choices

Sign out in Settings, clear the cache in Settings, or uninstall the app to remove local data. You can also end the session from Telegram > Settings > Devices.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13.

Changes and contact
